# Organization Module Future Updates & Roadmap

This document outlines future enhancements for multi-tenancy, custom roles, and enterprise governance in the **Organization Module**.

---

## 1. Planned Roadmap

1. **Custom RBAC & Permission Sets**: Enable enterprise customers to create custom roles (e.g. `BillingManager`, `ReleaseEngineer`) with fine-grained granular permission bits.
2. **Hierarchical Multi-Workspace Groups**: Support parent enterprise organizations with multiple sub-workspaces sharing central billing.
3. **Automated Domain Discovery (DNS TXT Verification)**: Automated verification of corporate domains via DNS TXT records for automatic SSO enforcement.
