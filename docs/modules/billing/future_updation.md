# Billing Module Future Updates & Roadmap

This document outlines upcoming billing capabilities, multi-currency support, and enterprise invoicing features for the **Billing Module**.

---

## 1. Planned Enhancements

1. **Custom Enterprise Contracts & Invoicing**: Support for offline Net-30/Net-60 invoicing via Stripe Invoicing API.
2. **Multi-Currency Localized Pricing**: Dynamic currency pricing (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY) based on customer billing country.
3. **Usage Threshold Email Warnings**: Automated notifications sent when organizations hit 80%, 90%, and 100% of their monthly event quotas.
